\chapter{Environment Variables}
\label{appendix:env-vars}

The full table of environment variables read by the backend, with defaults and the consequence of leaving each unset.

\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{>{\ttfamily\small}p{3.6cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.5cm} X}
  \toprule
  Variable & Default / required & Effect when unset \\
  \midrule
  \texttt{JWT\_SECRET}          & required, $\geq$32 chars & process exits via \texttt{log.Fatal}. \\
  \texttt{DB\_HOST}             & \texttt{localhost}        & connects to localhost. \\
  \texttt{DB\_PORT}             & \texttt{5432}             & default Postgres port. \\
  \texttt{DB\_USER}             & \texttt{postgres}         & default Compose user. \\
  \texttt{DB\_PASSWORD}         & \texttt{postgres}         & default Compose password. \\
  \texttt{DB\_NAME}             & \texttt{apex}             & default DB. \\
  \texttt{JUDGE0\_URL}          & \texttt{https://ce.judge0.com} & uses public Judge0 (rate-limited). \\
  \texttt{APP\_URL}             & \texttt{http://localhost:5173} & used in password reset links. \\
  \texttt{GOOGLE\_CLIENT\_ID}   & empty                     & Google sign-in disabled (\texttt{503} from \texttt{POST /auth/google}). \\
  \texttt{SMTP\_HOST}           & empty                     & email sending silently no-ops; in-app notifications still work. \\
  \texttt{SMTP\_PORT}           & empty / 587              & ignored if host empty. \\
  \texttt{SMTP\_USER}           & empty                     & ignored if host empty. \\
  \texttt{SMTP\_PASSWORD}       & empty                     & ignored if host empty. \\
  \texttt{SMTP\_FROM}           & empty                     & ignored if host empty. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}

The frontend reads only \texttt{VITE\_API\_URL} (default \texttt{/api}), which is set by the Vite proxy in development and by the reverse proxy in production.

\section{Sample \texttt{.env}}

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\footnotesize,breaklines=true]
# Required
JWT_SECRET=replace-with-32-or-more-random-characters

# Database
DB_HOST=localhost
DB_PORT=5432
DB_USER=postgres
DB_PASSWORD=postgres
DB_NAME=apex

# Judge0 (self-host strongly recommended for production)
JUDGE0_URL=https://ce.judge0.com

# App URL (used in password-reset email links)
APP_URL=http://localhost:5173

# Optional: Google sign-in
# GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx.apps.googleusercontent.com

# Optional: SMTP
# SMTP_HOST=smtp.example.com
# SMTP_PORT=587
# SMTP_USER=apex@example.com
# SMTP_PASSWORD=...
# SMTP_FROM=APEX <apex@example.com>
\end{lstlisting}
