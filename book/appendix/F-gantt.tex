\section{Phase notes}

\paragraph{Phase A --- Foundations.} Project setup, Go module layout, database connection, JWT middleware, signup/login.

\paragraph{Phase B --- Exam delivery.} Exam builder, problem and test-case editors, the take screen with Monaco, autosave, submit.

\paragraph{Phase C --- Grading.} Judge0 integration, the three graders, the finalisation arithmetic, the manual-grading queue.

\paragraph{Phase D --- Hardening.} Idempotent migrations, the autosave + reset-at workflow, the security review and the four patches in \Cref{ch:security}.

\paragraph{Phase E --- Evaluation.} \texttt{go test} and Vitest fixtures, the latency benchmark, the SUS user study.

\paragraph{Phase F --- Writing.} Producing this book and the defence presentation.
