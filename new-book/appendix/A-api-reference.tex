\chapter{API Reference}\label{ch:ap-a}

This appendix enumerates every HTTP route registered by the APEX backend. The tables are transcribed directly from the thirteen \texttt{RegisterRoutes} functions wired up in \texttt{main.go} (packages \texttt{auth}, \texttt{class}, \texttt{student}, \texttt{exam}, \texttt{problem}, \texttt{testcase}, \texttt{submission}, \texttt{execute}, \texttt{notification}, \texttt{profile}, \texttt{teacher}, \texttt{announcement}, \texttt{folder}); the \texttt{student} module additionally mounts three handlers from the \texttt{exam} package. Endpoints are grouped by the access level enforced through the layered middleware described in \cref{fig:authz}.

\section{Access model}

Every route except the liveness probe is mounted under one of two Gin groups declared in \texttt{main.go}:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \texttt{public := r.Group("/api")} --- no authentication; open to anonymous callers.
  \item \texttt{protected := r.Group("/api")} with \texttt{middleware.Auth(cfg.JWTSecret)} --- requires a valid \texttt{Authorization: Bearer <jwt>} header (HS256, 24-hour expiry). A failed or missing token yields \textbf{401}.
\end{itemize}

Within the protected group, feature subgroups add \texttt{middleware.RequireRole("teacher")} or \texttt{("student")} as a second gate (\textbf{403} on mismatch). A third gate --- resource ownership --- is enforced inside the handlers themselves and is not visible in the route table. The base URL in development is \texttt{http://localhost:8080/api}.

Four protected modules (\texttt{notification}, \texttt{profile}, \texttt{announcement}, \texttt{folder}) register their routes without an explicit \texttt{RequireRole} call. For \texttt{notification} and \texttt{profile} this is deliberate --- both roles own notifications and a profile. For \texttt{announcement} and \texttt{folder} the operations are teacher-facing and rely on handler-level ownership checks (\texttt{teacher\_id} scoping) rather than a role gate; they are listed under the teacher section below for clarity, with that caveat noted.

\section{Public}

No authentication. All paths live under the \texttt{public.Group("/auth")} subgroup registered by the \texttt{auth} module.

\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{l >{\ttfamily\small\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{5.6cm} X}
  \toprule
  Method & Path & Description \\
  \midrule
  POST & \texttt{/api/auth/signup}          & Create a user (email, password $\geq$6 chars, name, role); returns a JWT. \\
  POST & \texttt{/api/auth/login}           & Email + password login; returns a JWT or the generic ``invalid email or password''. \\
  POST & \texttt{/api/auth/forgot-password} & Request a password reset. Always \textbf{200} (no email enumeration); logs/sends a single-use token with a 1-hour expiry. \\
  POST & \texttt{/api/auth/reset-password}  & Consume the reset token and set a new password. \\
  POST & \texttt{/api/auth/google}          & Exchange a Google Identity Services credential (\texttt{id\_token}, optional \texttt{role}) for a JWT; \textbf{503} if \texttt{GOOGLE\_CLIENT\_ID} is unset. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}

\section{Protected: shared}

Any authenticated user (either role). Auth required; no role gate.

\begin{xltabular}{\linewidth}{l >{\ttfamily\small\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{5.6cm} X}
  \toprule
  Method & Path & Description \\
  \midrule
  DELETE & \texttt{/api/auth/account}               & Permanently delete the calling user's account via a role-aware cascade in one DB transaction. \\
  GET    & \texttt{/api/profile}                    & Read the calling user's profile. \\
  PUT    & \texttt{/api/profile}                    & Update profile fields (bio, avatar, notification toggles, teacher defaults). \\
  PUT    & \texttt{/api/profile/password}           & Change the calling user's password. \\
  GET    & \texttt{/api/notifications}              & List the caller's notifications. \\
  PUT    & \texttt{/api/notifications/:id/read}     & Mark one notification read. \\
  PUT    & \texttt{/api/notifications/read-all}     & Mark all of the caller's notifications read. \\
  GET    & \texttt{/api/notifications/unread-count} & Unread count for the notification bell. \\
  POST   & \texttt{/api/submissions/run}            & Run a candidate solution against the problem's sample test cases; no \texttt{Submission} is persisted. \\
  GET    & \texttt{/api/submissions/:id}            & Read a submission; ownership re-checked (student owner or exam-owning teacher). \\
  POST   & \texttt{/api/execute}                    & Standalone code execution (playground); no persistence. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{xltabular}

The two run/execute endpoints and the submission read are the shared surface between the coding UI paths for both roles.
